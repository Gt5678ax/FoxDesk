# Community code of conduct

FoxDesk is built in public. We want issues, discussions, and pull requests to be
useful to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique the change, not the person.
- Share enough context for others to reproduce a problem.
- Respect privacy and remove customer data, credentials, and personal information.
- Accept that maintainers may close work that is outside the self-hosted scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
publishing private information, or repeated bad-faith participation are not
accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `admin@aenze.com`. Reports will be handled
with discretion. This address is for conduct reports, not security disclosures.
Use [private vulnerability reporting](SECURITY.md) for security issues.
